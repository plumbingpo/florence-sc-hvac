Keeping Your Columbia HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners last 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as climbing utility bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is almost always cheaper and far less stressful than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Small problems rarely stay small for long. Take a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and just like that a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we ask homeowners to keep our number in their phone and reach out at the first sign of trouble. Catching it early saves money and spares you a miserable night.
